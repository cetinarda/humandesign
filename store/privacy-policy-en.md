# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 16 May 2026
**App:** Sakin Tasarım (`life.sakin.tasarim`)

Sakin Tasarım is a Human Design app that runs entirely on your device. Your birth data never leaves your phone. This policy explains, in plain English, what data we touch, why we touch it, and what rights you have under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).

## 1. Data Controller

- **Name:** cetinarda / sakin.life
- **Website:** https://sakin.life
- **Privacy email:** privacy@sakin.life
- **General contact:** info@sakin.life

For GDPR purposes we act as the data controller. Because no data leaves your device, there is no data processor or sub-processor.

## 2. Data We Collect

To draw your bodygraph we ask for the following, which you type in yourself:

- **A name or nickname** (so you can recognize the profile)
- **Date of birth** (day, month, year)
- **Time of birth** (hour, minute)
- **Birthplace** (city name as text — no GPS, no map permission)

We collect nothing else. No phone number, no email, no IP address, no device identifier, no advertising ID, no location permission, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

The birth data is used for exactly one purpose: computing your Human Design chart **on your device**. Planetary positions are calculated offline by the app's CPU. No request is sent to any server, ever.

We do not use the data for marketing, profiling, advertising, or analytics. The legal basis under GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) is performance of the service you asked for, and the data is processed locally.

## 4. Where Data Is Stored

All data lives in your device's own local storage (AsyncStorage on iOS/Android, LocalStorage in the browser for the web version). It is:

- **Not** uploaded to our servers.
- **Not** backed up to our cloud (your device's own OS backup — iCloud, Google Backup — may copy it; that is under your Apple/Google account control, not ours).
- Stored as ordinary app data. Because it never leaves the device, we do not apply our own encryption layer on top of the OS's storage encryption.

## 5. Sharing With Third Parties

**We share no data with any third party.** No ad network, no analytics vendor, no cloud provider, no social platform, no CRM. Since the data does not leave your device, there is nothing to share.

## 6. Analytics and Tracking

The app contains **zero analytics and zero tracking SDKs.** No Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Meta SDK, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no App Center. No cookies, no pixels, no fingerprinting.

Per Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, we do not track you across apps or websites and we do not show the ATT prompt because we have nothing to ask about.

## 7. Ads and Purchases

The app is **ad-free.** There are no in-app purchases. There is no subscription. Therefore we collect no payment information, billing address, or wallet data.

## 8. Children

Sakin Tasarım is rated 17+ and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a parent or guardian finds the app on a child's device, uninstalling the app removes every byte of data.

## 9. Your Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under EU and UK data protection law you have the right to:

- **Access** — all your data is visible inside the app itself.
- **Rectification** — edit a profile any time.
- **Erasure** — delete a profile or uninstall the app to remove everything.
- **Portability** — export is on the roadmap; today you can copy the visible fields manually.
- **Object** / **Restrict** — uninstall the app; we have no server processing to restrict.
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local supervisory authority (e.g. KVKK in Turkey, ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

You do not need to file a formal request to use these rights; the app exposes them directly. If you'd like to write us anyway, email privacy@sakin.life and we will reply within 30 days.

## 10. Your Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident:

- We do **not** sell or share your personal information.
- We do **not** use sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics.
- We collect none of the categories of personal information that would require a "Do Not Sell or Share" link.

You still have rights to know, delete, correct and limit; all of these are exercised inside the app itself. There is no need to verify your identity to us because we hold no copy of your data.

## 11. International Transfers

There are no international transfers — the data does not travel.

## 12. Security

Because data never traverses the network, there is no exposure to interception or server-side breach. The only attack surface is physical access to your device, so we recommend keeping a screen lock and biometric authentication enabled.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes and on https://sakin.life/tasarim/gizlilik. The effective date at the top will always reflect the latest version.
