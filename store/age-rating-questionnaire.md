# App Store Connect — Age Rating Questionnaire

A field-by-field answer guide for the Age Rating questionnaire in App Store Connect
(App Information → Age Rating → Edit).

The goal: **17+** in the United States (matches Apple's expectation for esoteric/spiritual
content with adult themes), **12+** acceptable for Turkey when an alternative regional rating is allowed.

---

## Cartoon or Fantasy Violence

**Answer: None**

The app has no characters, combat, weapons or violence imagery. The bodygraph is a static
diagram of nine geometric shapes.

## Realistic Violence

**Answer: None**

Same as above. No imagery depicting violence.

## Prolonged Graphic or Sadistic Realistic Violence

**Answer: None**

## Profanity or Crude Humor

**Answer: None**

The interpretive text is written in calm, neutral Turkish/English. No swearing, no crude humor.

## Mature/Suggestive Themes

**Answer: Infrequent/Mild**

Reasoning: Human Design discusses concepts such as relationship dynamics, intimacy patterns,
emotional shadow material and "not-self" psychology. Nothing sexual or graphic is depicted,
but the framing is adult/introspective. Selecting "Infrequent/Mild" is the honest answer and
the most reliable path to a 17+ rating without overclaiming content.

## Horror/Fear Themes

**Answer: None**

No horror imagery, no scary content.

## Medical/Treatment Information

**Answer: None**

The app contains an explicit disclaimer that it is **not** medical advice and provides no
diagnostic, treatment or therapeutic claims. Because no medical information is presented,
this question should be answered "None". The disclaimer language is reproduced in
`app-review-notes.md` and is visible in the app.

## Unrestricted Web Access

**Answer: No**

The app does not embed a general-purpose web browser. The only outbound links are deep

## Expected outcome

- **Apple (App Store):** 17+
- **Google Play (IARC):** Mature 17+ or "Teen" depending on regional mapping; declare the same answers and accept the IARC outcome.
- **Turkey (if a localized rating control is offered):** 12+ is acceptable; the esoteric/introspective tone is the only mild gate.

## Why 17+ and not 12+

The conservative choice is 17+. Reasons:

1. Human Design discusses adult psychological themes (shadow patterns, relational dynamics,
   ego/will, surrender) that may not be appropriate for younger users without context.
2. A 17+ rating reduces the risk that a reviewer flags the app as "unsuitable for children
   exposed to esoteric belief systems" — a documented edge case in App Review.
3. The app is positioned for personal-development adults; setting 17+ aligns the audience.

## Where to paste this

- App Store Connect → App Information → Age Rating → Edit
- For Google Play, use the IARC questionnaire in Play Console with the same answers.

## Reviewer hint

If a reviewer queries the "Infrequent/Mild Mature/Suggestive Themes" answer, the response is:
"The app references adult psychological and relational concepts in its Human Design content.
There is no nudity, no sexual content, no graphic imagery."
